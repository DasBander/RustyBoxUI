# Talus artwork

The menu painting `copper-narrows.webp` was generated for this example with the built-in ImageGen tool. The game draws its terrain, river, glider and gates as WebGL geometry and synthesizes its sounds with Web Audio.

The menu uses Barlow Condensed and Inter under the SIL Open Font License. Their notices accompany the fonts in the distribution.

## Menu art prompt

Landscape 16:9 original key art for Talus, a canyon gliding game. A single small piloted graphite delta-wing glider with an ivory leading edge and a bright vermilion wing panel banks in the right-hand third of the picture over a winding blue-green river. Towering red sandstone cliffs catch low peach-gold sunlight. The left third is a continuous, deeply shadowed near canyon wall with quiet texture so an actual game menu can sit over it. Layered mesa silhouettes dissolve into a pale apricot horizon. Cinematic, painterly 3D game concept art with bold sculpted rock planes, fine atmospheric grain, believable scale and a restrained outdoor-adventure palette. Wide edge-to-edge setting. No words, letters, logos, interface, weapons or watermarks.
